## Design direction agreed with the user

Basement Farm should feel like a grounded first-person survival/RPG game, not
a prototype room full of cubes or text-heavy debug prompts. It is a cold
post-apocalyptic basement where the player wakes, keeps a furnace alive,
cultures insects, hunts blind slimes in a full separate hunt room, processes
the loot, and eventually fills orders. The room needs purposeful, rugged props
and intuitive HUD icons/arrows rather than big blocks of instructions.

Important constraints:

- Existing generated models must come from the Basement-owned Blender pipeline
  in `assets/models/basement/source/generation_scripts/`, not ad-hoc runtime
  placeholder geometry where a durable prop is needed.
- New visual work should fit the rusty timber/metal/soot palette already used
  by the basement, furnace, workbench, and cave assets.
- Work in small playable milestones and ask the user to check each one before
  starting the next story beat.
- Do not advance the door/creature/chain/butchering-order milestone yet.
